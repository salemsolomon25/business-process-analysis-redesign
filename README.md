# Business Process Analysis and Redesign
Business process analysis and redesign project for Waddell Entertainment Consultants, including process mapping, workflow analysis, CRM evaluation, requirements gathering, process improvement recommendations, and implementation planning.

## Overview

Capstone project focused on analyzing and redesigning business processes for Waddell Entertainment Consultants, LLC.

The project evaluated operational challenges related to event registration, membership management, data management, and continuing legal education (CLE) credit tracking. A future-state solution was proposed using a CRM platform to improve efficiency, automation, reporting, and scalability.

## Business Challenges Identified

- Event and membership registration inefficiencies
- Manual data management processes
- Limited reporting capabilities
- Inefficient CLE credit tracking
- Manual workflows with high risk of error

## Responsibilities

As Data Manager, I:

- Analyzed existing business processes
- Developed current-state process documentation
- Quantified process inefficiencies
- Created workflow diagrams
- Evaluated process improvement opportunities
- Assisted with future-state business process design
- Contributed to CRM solution evaluation

## Proposed Solution

The team selected Odoo CRM as the recommended platform due to:

- Centralized data management
- Event management capabilities
- Membership management features
- CLE tracking functionality
- Scalability
- Lower operating costs
